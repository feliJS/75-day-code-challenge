# Challenge 5: Emoji Translator 😎

What if you could communicate using only emojis?

Build a translator that converts ordinary words and sentences into emoji-filled messages!

How creative can your translator get?

## 😁 Your Challenge

Create an application that replaces recognized words with corresponding emojis.

### Requirements
- Accept a sentence or paragraph as input.
- Create a dictionary of at least 20 words and their corresponding emojis.
- Detect words that have matching emojis.
- Replace matching words with emojis.
- Keep unrecognized words unchanged.
- Handle uppercase and lowercase words.
- Display the translated result.

### ✨ Bonus Ideas
- Support multiple emojis for the same word.
- Let users create custom emoji dictionaries.
- Add a reverse translation mode.
- Preserve punctuation and formatting.
- Include different translation styles.
- Allow users to copy or share their translated text.

## 💡 Example

**Input:**

```text
I love cats and pizza!
```

**Possible output:**

```text
I ❤️ 🐱 and 🍕!
```
